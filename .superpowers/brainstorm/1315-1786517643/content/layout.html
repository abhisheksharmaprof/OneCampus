Finance suite layout — which structure works better?

Both keep all finance features in one tab. The difference is how sections are organized and how much room the work area gets.

One horizontal nav, but sections grouped by workflow: Collect (Overview · Invoices · Payments & Receipts · Dues) | Setup (Fee Plans · Templates · Settings) | Operations (Expenses · Payroll · Budget · Reports, unchanged). Full width for tables.
